🥐 Freshly Made Snacks

Where It All Started

Wittycha began with a simple idea: creating a cozy destination where people could enjoy premium matcha while feeling relaxed, inspired, and at home. We wanted to build something more than just a café. A place where small moments become memorable experiences, where every drink feels comforting, and where happiness can be found in the little things.

💌 Merchandise Corner

The Heart Of Wittycha

Matcha represents calmness, balance, and comfort. Its rich flavor and vibrant green color became the inspiration behind our brand identity. Every cup is carefully crafted to create a smooth and enjoyable experience that helps our customers slow down, recharge, and enjoy the moment.

🌿 Cozy Café Moments

Why Wittycha?

The name Wittycha reflects creativity, warmth, and our love for matcha. We wanted a name that feels playful yet comforting, representing the cheerful atmosphere we hope every customer experiences. It reminds us that happiness often comes from simple things: a delicious drink, a cozy space, and meaningful moments shared together.
